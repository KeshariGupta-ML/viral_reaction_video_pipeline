Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Viral Video Publisher application ("App") accesses and manages data when interacting with Meta's Graph API, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Pages.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The App only accesses user data explicitly required to facilitate content generation and social publishing, including:

  • Target Facebook Page IDs and linked Instagram Business Account IDs.
  • Publicly staged video media URLs and descriptive captions provided by the account owner.

The App does not sell, harvest, or share personal data with external third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not store personal identifiable information (PII) beyond what is needed to execute scheduled publishing sessions. Users can request data deletion or revoke application permissions at any time via their Meta Account Settings.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact the developer through your project GitHub repository issues page.
